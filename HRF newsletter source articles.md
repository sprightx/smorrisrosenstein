## Hind Rajab’s Alleged Killer Is in the United States. HRF Demands His Arrest.

**Category:** Legal Action  
**Source:** https://www.hindrajabfoundation.org/posts/hind-rajabs-alleged-killer-is-in-new-york-hrf-demands-his-arrest

His alleged responsibility extends across Gaza: attacks on Al-Shifa Hospital, the destruction of the UNRWA headquarters, the razing of entire neighbourhoods, and the destruction of the Rafah Crossing—devastation he publicly celebrated. _

## HRF Files ICC Complaint Against Israeli Medical Association, Doctors, and Officials Over Role in Sde Teiman Torture

**Published:** October 7th 2026  
**Category:** Legal Action  
**Source:** https://www.hindrajabfoundation.org/posts/hrf-files-article-15-communication-with-the-icc-on-medical-complicity-in-the-torture-of-palestinians-at-sde-teiman--filed-as-the-israeli-medical-association-attends-the-world-medical-association-general-assembly-in-rotterdam

**8 October 2026 – Brussels, Belgium/Rotterdam, the Netherlands** – The Hind Rajab Foundation (HRF) has filed a communication under **Article 15 of the Rome Statute** with the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court (ICC) calling for an investigation into the involvement of Israeli Ministry of Health and Ministry of Defense officials, the Israeli Medical Association, medical professionals, and hospital administrators in the death, torture, and ill-treatment of Palestinian detainees held at the **Sde Teiman detention facility** since 7 October 2023.

The submission argues that the conduct documented in the filing amounts to **war crimes** under Articles 8(2)(a)(i)–(iii) and 8(2)(b)(xxi), **crimes against humanity** under Article 7(1)(a), (f), (h), and (k), and **acts of genocide** under Article 6(a) and (b) of the Rome Statute.

### **Medical Ethics in Rotterdam: The WMA Faces Its Own Test**

The filing was timed by HRF to coincide with the convening of the **World Medical Association (WMA) General Assembly** in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, from 7 to 10 October 2026.

The Israeli Medical Association (IMA) represents over 90% of physicians in Israel, including Israeli doctors assigned to work at Sde Teiman, and is a founding member of the WMA. IMA leadership also holds positions within the WMA’s governing structures with IMA President Professor** Zion Hagay** chairing the WMA’s Socio-Medical Affairs Committee and IMA Chief Executive Officer Advocate** Leah Wapner** serving in several advisory roles, including on the WMA’s Finance and Planning, Socio-Medical Affairs, and Medical Ethics Committees.

The WMA General Assembly is **convening to debate medical ethics** while members of the IMA, which has provided institutional cover for torture and medical abuse of Palestinian detainees, sit among its delegates with impunity. The meeting also comes amid growing pressure on the WMA to act, with more than 1,300 health professionals and organizations **calling on the WMA to suspend the IMA** in a petition published in [_The Lancet_](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(26)01185-2/fulltext) in June 2026.

### **The Sde Teiman Field Hospital: Medical Complicity in a System of Torture**

Since October 2023, Sde Teiman, a military base in the Negev desert, was used to detain thousands of Palestinians from Gaza, the majority of whom were civilians, including men, women, children, the elderly, journalists, and healthcare and humanitarian workers. The detainees were held under the invented designation of “unlawful combatants,” a legal fiction used to strip detainees of basic legal protections and enabling their detention without charge or trial.

Just over a week after 7 October 2023, the Israeli Ministry of Health established a medical facility inside the Sde Teiman detention complex. What began as a medical tent was expanded into a field hospital consisting of tents with between eight and twenty beds each, built without tender by K.L.P. Global Ltd., an Israeli private company that also exports ‘defense’ systems.

Despite the fact that the field hospital was operating inside a military detention complex, it remained under the auspices of the Israeli Ministry of Health. However, its medical staff, including civilian doctors called up as reservists and volunteers, were required to sign documents placing them under the authority of Israel’s Military Police rather than its Medical Corps.

_Inside Sde Teiman Detention Center. Credit: Breaking the Silence_

On 19 December 2023, **Israel’s Ministry of Health issued a directive that** **formally integrated the medical facility into Sde Teiman’s system of torture and abuse**, imposing the following requirements:

- Instructing medical staff to treat detainees while handcuffed and blindfolded;

- Prohibiting staff from identifying themselves by name and requiring them to sign medical records using only professional titles. (This practice obscures individual medical responsibility and prevents injuries sustained in custody from being documented in an accountable clinical record subject to legal scrutiny);

- Restricting transfers to civilian hospitals to cases involving an imminent threat to life or risk of irreversible disability; and,

- Authorizing forced medical treatment without safeguards or protections.

**Physicians, nurses, and paramedics who served at the field hospital, together with former detainees, have documented the abuses carried out within this system.**

In April 2024, a doctor who worked at Sde Teiman wrote to the Israeli Attorney General and Ministers of Defense and Health, reporting that two detainees had undergone leg amputations within a single week as a result of injuries caused by shackling, which he described as routine.

Medical workers also informed the Associated Press that they witnessed surgeries performed without adequate pain management, including on an elderly detainee who underwent leg surgery while “screaming and shaking.” Others described detainees forced to wear diapers, bound by all four limbs to hospital beds, and blindfolded.

By November 2025,** at least 29 detainees had died** at Sde Teiman as a result of physical violence, medical neglect, or both.

### **The Israeli Medical Association: Legitimizing a System of Torture **

The submission devotes a chapter specifically to the role of the IMA in legitimizing the abuse and torture carried out at Sde Teiman.

The IMA’s response to allegations of medical complicity has been limited to generic reminders of physicians’ ethical obligations, while the findings of an internal ethical assessment conducted by the Ministry of Health following a visit to Sde Teiman have never been disclosed.

Rather than investigate the allegations, IMA leadership publicly defended the medical care provided at Sde Teiman.

On 24 April 2024, IMA President **Professor Zion Hagay**, IMA Ethics Board Chair **Dr. Yossi Walfisch**, and **Malke Borow** of the IMA Division of Law and Policy published [a letter](https://ima-contentfiles.s3.amazonaws.com/24042024LetterToTheEditorAJOB.pdf) in _The American Journal of Bioethics_ asserting that detainees at Sde Teiman were “receiving appropriate medical care.” The letter also referred to Palestinian detainees who had neither been tried nor convicted as “Hamas terrorists;” and, it specifically invoked Walfisch’s own visit to Sde Teiman as evidence:

> “_One of us [Walfisch] has visited the IDF medical facility as part of a delegation of medical ethics and professional experts and can attest to the fact that the prisoners there are receiving appropriate medical care within the necessary precautions taken to ensure the safety of the medical staff._”

The letter’s assurances directly contradict standards reaffirmed by the **IMA’s own Ethics Bureau** in September 2023, where it reiterated that routine restraint during medical treatment is prohibited, specifically identifying cross-binding, plastic restraints, and covering patients' eyes.

The **IMA initiated no disciplinary proceedings** against members implicated in conduct violating its code of ethics. Instead, its leadership used an international bioethics journal to evade accountability and obscure practices Israeli physicians, whistleblowers, former detainees, and UN investigators have documented as torture and medical abuse.

### **Individual Criminal Responsibility**

The Article 15 submission calls on the ICC Prosecutor to investigate a chain of responsibility extending from senior political officials who designed the policy and officials who issued binding directives, to the military commanders, medical professionals, and professional bodies that implemented or legitimized the system.

Those identified include former Ministers of Health **Moshe Arbel** and **Uriel Buso**, IMA President **Professor Zion Hagay**, IMA Chief Executive Officer **Advocate Leah Wapner**, and IMA Ethics Board Chair **Dr. Yosef Walfisch**, among others named in the communication.

#### ** Dyab Abou Jahjah, Director General at HRF:**

> “_This week, the Israeli medical establishment takes its seat at the World Medical Association General Assembly in Rotterdam as though nothing has happened. The same association that called the torture of Palestinian detainees “appropriate medical care” now sits among delegates debating medical ethics. Israeli physicians abandoned their neutrality when they agreed to treat patients in chains and erase what was done to them . The WMA must answer why an association that provided institutional cover for torture is still welcome at its table._”

#### **Natacha Bracq, Head of Litigation at HRF:**

> “_The evidence shows that medical participation in torture and abuse at Sde Teiman was structural. The Israeli Ministry of Health built the field hospital, dictated the terms of medical treatment; doctors served under Military Police authority rather than the Medical Corps; and a professional association provided ethical cover. The medical system kept detainees alive to endure further torture and interrogation while ensuring their injuries could not be traced through clinical records. HRF’s Article 15 submission calls on the ICC Prosecutor to investigate this system for what it is: a chain of complicity in torture extending from government ministers to practicing physicians._”

The WMA and national medical associations also have been notified and received a copy of HRF's Article 15 Communication.

**Today’s submission is only the beginning.**

HRF is preparing files on Israeli medical professionals with dual nationalities who participated directly in Israel's genocide in Gaza. We will bring each case before national prosecutors in every jurisdiction where these individuals hold citizenship.

## Polish Prosecutor Declines to Investigate Alleged Involvement of Nitro-Chem in Genocide

**Published:** October 19th 2026  
**Category:** Legal Action  
**Source:** https://www.hindrajabfoundation.org/posts/polish-prosecutor-declines-to-investigate-alleged-involvement-of-nitro-chem-in-genocide-

**The Hind Rajab Foundation (HRF), the International Federation for Human Rights (FIDH), and the European Legal Support Center (ELSC) have appealed the Warsaw District Prosecutor’s decision to dismiss their criminal complaint against representatives of Nitro-Chem S.A., one of the world's largest TNT producers and a major supplier to United States (U.S.) and NATO weapons manufacturers.**

**19 October 2026 - Warsaw, Poland **— The coalition filed its original complaint on 26 June 2026, alleging that representatives of Nitro-Chem aided international crimes in Gaza—including genocide, war crimes, and crimes against humanity—by supplying TNT and other explosives both directly to Israel and to the U.S. arms manufacturers for use in the production of heavy aerial bombs and artillery shells. The U.S. subsequently supplied large quantities of such munitions to Israel for use in its ongoing genocide against the Palestinians.

### **Six-week dismissal without a full investigation**

The Prosecutor’s decision, dated 3 August 2026, was issued just six weeks after the complaint was filed—a remarkably short timeframe for a case involving complex international supply chains, cross-border transfers, and allegations of grave international crimes. The swift dismissal reflects a failure to conduct the thorough, diligent investigation required by both Polish criminal procedure and Poland’s obligations under the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide, the Geneva Conventions and the European Convention on Human Rights.

Key factual questions—including Nitro-Chem’s contractual relationships, knowledge of the weapons programs for which its TNT was supplied, communications with customers, and the subsequent transfer and use of relevant weapons in the genocide—should have been investigated before the complaint could properly be dismissed.

### **U.S.-supplied bombs and their consequences in Gaza**

According to the report _“_[_The Missing Ingredient: Polish TNT_](https://www.embargoforpalestine.com/reports)_”,_ the U.S. has supplied Israel with large quantities of aerial bombs and other heavy munitions used in the genocide committed against the Palestinians in Gaza. These include Mk-80 series bombs, such as the Mk-82 and Mk-84, as well as BLU-109 bunker buster bombs. Between October 2023 and July 2024 alone, the U.S. transferred at least **14,000 Mk-84 bombs, 6,500 Mk-82 bombs, and 1,000 BLU-109 bunker buster bombs **to Israel. The coalition alleges that some of these weapons may have contained TNT supplied by Nitro-Chem.

These are high-explosive, wide-area-of-effect weapons with a blast radius extending across hundreds of meters; their use in densely populated civilian areas breaches fundamental principles of international humanitarian law regarding distinction, proportionality, and precaution.

### **Evidence points to Nitro-Chem’s central role in the supply chain**

The report further documents how Nitro-Chem became the linchpin of this supply chain. Because the U.S ceased domestic TNT production in 1986, it relies heavily on imports, with Polish officials acknowledging that roughly **90% of U.S.-imported TNT comes from Poland**. General Dynamics, the primary U.S. manufacturer of these munitions, explicitly identified Nitro-Chem as “**the only qualified TNT source for U.S. Bomb Programs**” in a 2018 presentation.

According to the report, this dependency has persisted through the ongoing genocide. Despite the ICJ’s finding of a plausible risk of genocide in January 2024, ICC arrest warrants against Israeli leaders in November 2024, and public knowledge of the U.S. supply of bombs to Israel, contracts between Nitro-Chem and U.S. arms manufacturers were renewed and expanded repeatedly after October 2023, including **new deals in April 2024 and April 2025 worth approximately USD 310 million.**

Furthermore, records confirm that Nitro-Chem sold explosives directly to Israeli arms manufacturers such as IMI, Elbit, and Rafael.

The continuation and expansion of these commercial relationships, in the face of mounting evidence concerning the use and consequences of the relevant weapons in Gaza, require a thorough and genuine investigation into Nitro-Chem representatives’ knowledge and intent.

### **Coalition seeks judicial review**

HRF, FIDH, and ELSC are asking the Warsaw Regional Court to review the Prosecutor’s dismissal and ensure that the allegations are subject to an effective and independent investigation.

**Natacha Bracq, Head of Litigation at HRF**

> “_Our complaint highlights a supply chain that runs through Poland extensively documented in “The Missing Ingredient: Polish TNT”_ _, and yet the Polish Prosecutor chose to close the case before even uncovering how far it reaches. We are asking the Prosecutor to investigate the contracts, shipments, communications and chain of responsibility that only Polish authorities are in a position to fully uncover. A six-week dismissal cannot substitute for an investigation into whether Polish-made TNT contributed to weapons used to commit international crimes. If there is nothing to uncover, let the evidence establish that. But if this supply chain helped enable the genocide in Gaza, Poland has a responsibility to find it, expose it, and stop it._”

**Clémence Bectarte, coordinator of the Litigation Action Group at the FIDH**

> “_This case tests whether Poland will follow the example of other states now taking seriously their duty to investigate the genocide in Gaza, including economic actors. The weapons used in Gaza do not appear from nowhere — they are manufactured, financed, and supplied. Ignoring that chain means ignoring how this genocide is made possible._”

**Daan de Grefte, Senior Legal Officer at the ELSC**

> “_This case is a clear example of how corporate and state interests try to dilute criminal responsibility for the crime of genocide. Genocide - and its related war crimes - is not committed only by the Zionist regime. It is committed by a global network of politicians and CEOs, and enabled by double standards and a failing rule of law. It is the responsibility of law enforcement authorities to uphold their own criminal legislation, even if they are violated by 'close allies' or strategically important corporations. We are confident that the Warsaw Regional Court will not shy away from its responsibility to uphold Polish law._”

The organizations remain committed to pursuing accountability for individuals and companies alleged to have contributed to international crimes and to ensuring that credible allegations are properly investigated by the competent authorities.

## HRF Files UK Criminal Complaint Against Israeli Soldier Over Destruction of Food Facility Amid Gaza Starvation

**Published:** October 26th 2026  
**Category:** Legal Action  
**Source:** https://www.hindrajabfoundation.org/posts/the-hind-rajab-foundation-files-criminal-complaint-in-the-uk-against-israeli-soldier-lidor-dafna-for-war-crimes-and-acts-of-genocide-committed-in-gaza

**25 October 2026, Brussels, Belgium** – The Hind Rajab Foundation (HRF) has filed a criminal complaint with authorities in the United Kingdom (UK) against **Lidor Dafna**, an Israeli soldier serving in the 603rd Combat Engineering Battalion of the 7th Armored Brigade, for war crimes, crimes against humanity, and acts of genocide committed in the Gaza Strip.

Dafna serves as a **D9 armored bulldozer operator and, as a reservist, has taken part in operations in Gaza and Lebanon**, from November 2023 onwards, including in Gaza City, Khan Younis, and Rafah. HRF has reason to believe Dafna is currently in the United Kingdom.

### **The Destruction of the Great Ocean Mills and the Weaponization of Hunger**

Dafna documented his own deployment on social media. His posts place him in Gaza City in November 2023, where he photographed himself at the **Sarafand Boys School** holding a sign reading “Opening Soon – A new Branch of Sabich Hasharon,” an Israeli fast-food chain. He subsequently documented his unit's demolition operations in Khan Younis and Rafah. Dafna was later deployed to Lebanon.

The main allegation in HRF’s filing against Dafna focuses on the destruction of the **Great Ocean Mills**, an industrial flour-producing facility in the Morag Corridor between Khan Younis and Rafah, on 25 May 2025. Footage posted by Dafna himself shows a bulldozer demolishing the facility's wheat silos. In the weeks that followed, Israeli forces occupied the building and **turned it into as a military base and reportedly used it to shoot starving Palestinians** seeking food aid at a nearby Gaza Humanitarian Foundation (GHF) site. In the months that followed and by August 2025, the Israeli army killed at least 1,400 Palestinians seeking food aid near GHF sites.

At the time of its destruction, Israel had sealed the Gaza Strip to all food, fuel, and medicine. Flour was being sold for as much as US$70 per kilogram, and **half a million people in Gaza were facing starvation**.

_As Israel deliberately cut off food, fuel, and medicine to Gaza, Israeli soldier Lidor Dafna participated in the destruction of the Great Ocean Mills, a vital flour-producing facility, and publicly posted footage of its demolition._

### **The 603rd Battalion's Record of Destruction**

The 603rd Battalion has served as a core operational arm in Israel’s systematic destruction across the Gaza Strip. **HRF documented over 60 controlled demolitions of buildings**, including mosques and entire residential blocks, and the razing of entire communities that rendered large swathes of Gaza uninhabitable. The battalion also played a key role in establishing the Morag Corridor, a process accompanied by the widespread and systematic destruction of civilian infrastructure, agricultural land and the built environment.

### **Dafna’s Crimes**

HRF's investigation indicates that Dafna may be criminally responsible for war crimes, including:

- Extensive destruction of property not justified by military necessity;

- Intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects;

- Intentionally using starvation of civilians as a method of warfare;

- The crime against humanity of extermination; and

- The act of genocide consisting of deliberately inflicting on the Palestinian group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction, in whole or in part.

_Images posted by Israeli soldier Lidor Dafna himself, documenting his direct participation in military operations across Gaza as part of the 603rd Combat Engineering Battalion, implicated in the widespread destruction of Palestinian civilian infrastructure._

### **HRF’s Request**

HRF calls on the UK authorities to act urgently on this complaint: to **open an investigation, arrest and question Dafna without delay**, and ensure the UK does not serve as a safe haven by those implicated in committing international crimes in Gaza.

As the UK authorities are aware, Article 146 of the Fourth Geneva Convention imposes an express obligation on every High Contracting Party to “search for” persons alleged to have committed, or ordered, grave breaches of the Convention, irrespective of nationality. That obligation, reproduced in Schedule 4 to the Geneva Conventions Act 1957, is the treaty basis for the grave-breaches system of universal jurisdiction.

This obligation is currently being tested in real time. **An active Israeli soldier is currently walking free in London**, touring the city. This case further demonstrates the need to question all Israeli adults entering the country about their service in the Israeli army, and in particular about any deployment to Gaza and Lebanon, and arrest those suspected of international crimes where the evidence supports it.

#### **Dyab Abou Jahjah, General Director at HRF:**

> “_"Lidor Dafna filmed himself destroying one of the last remaining food production facilities serving a starving population, then posted the footage on social media as a trophy. The United Kingdom now faces a simple test: will it arrest and prosecute a man, who remains an active soldier of an army carrying out such crimes, or once again permit a perpetrator of genocide to walk freely on its soil and return to Gaza to continue committing his crimes? We have filed our complaint. Now it is up to the British authorities to act."_”

#### **Natacha Bracq, Head of Litigation at HRF:**

> “_"Lidor Dafna remains an active reservist in the very battalion that carried out this destruction and can be called back at any time to serve in Gaza or Lebanon, where his unit continues to operate. Under the Genocide Convention, the UK's duty to prevent genocide arises the moment it knows, or should know, of a serious risk of genocide. A soldier filmed destroying a starving population’s means of survival should never be free to board a plane much less return to military service. Allowing him to exit the country would be in breach of the UK’s international obligations."_”

HRF will continue to identify, document, and pursue those responsible for weaponizing hunger and destroying Palestinian lives, wherever they travel and wherever accountability can be sought. Crossing a border does not erase a crime. **Leaving Gaza does not extinguish responsibility. **We will follow the evidence, pursue the perpetrators, and challenge every safe haven until those responsible face justice.
